## Description
MRC Barbershop is the easiest way to book your haircut in Baltimore.

**FIND YOUR SHOP**
Two branches across Baltimore. See them on the map, pick the closest one.

**PICK YOUR BARBER**
Browse barber profiles — see their actual work photos, verified skills, and real customer reviews before you book.

**BOOK IN SECONDS**
Choose your service, day, and time. A $5 deposit locks your chair. Apple Pay accepted.

**SAVE WITH PLANS**
- Monthly Plan: 4 haircuts for $120/month
- Mix & Match: pick any 4 services in any order, save 25% every month
- Family plans: one account, multiple memberships

**GIFT A HAIRCUT**
Buy a haircut for your son, brother, or friend. They get a QR code — no app needed to redeem.

**WE MAKE IT RIGHT**
Not happy with your cut? Snap a photo in the app and we'll make it right with a free haircut.

**MRC ASSISTANT**
Built-in AI helper answers your questions — prices, hours, anything.

Download MRC Barbershop and look sharp every week.
