# App Store Connect — Privacy Labels (App Privacy)

## Data Collected

### Contact Info
- **Name**: Collected for booking identification. Linked to user. Not shared.
- **Email Address**: Collected for account login and booking confirmations. Linked to user. Not shared.

### Location
- **Precise Location**: Used to sort barbershop branches by distance. Optional (user can deny). Not stored on server. Not shared.

### Photos
- **Photos**: Profile photo (optional), haircut completion photos (barbers), complaint evidence photos (customers). Stored securely. Linked to user. Not shared with third parties.

### Financial Info
- **Payment Info**: Processed by Stripe (PCI-compliant). We never see or store card numbers. Used for booking deposits, plans, gifts, tips.

### Usage Data
- **Product Interaction**: Booking history for "My appointments". Linked to user. Not shared.

## Data Not Collected
- No advertising data
- No third-party advertising
- No data sold to anyone
- No tracking across apps

## User Rights
- Account deletion available in-app (Profile → Delete Account)
- Data export available on request
- Privacy Policy: https://mrc-barbershop-mrc-0043.vercel.app/privacy
