# App Store Screenshots — Specifications

## Required Sizes

### iPhone 6.7" (1290 × 2796) — REQUIRED
- iPhone 14 Pro Max, 15 Pro Max, 16 Pro Max
- Minimum 3 screenshots, maximum 10

### iPhone 6.5" (1242 × 2688) — REQUIRED
- iPhone 11 Pro Max, XS Max
- Minimum 3 screenshots, maximum 10

### iPad 12.9" (2048 × 2732) — Optional (if iPad supported)
- Skip if iPhone-only

## Recommended Screenshots (in order)

1. **Home/Branches** — Map with nearby barbershops, gold theme
   Caption: "Find barbershops near you"

2. **Barber Profile** — Photo, ratings, reviews, skills
   Caption: "Choose your barber"

3. **Booking** — Date/time picker with available slots
   Caption: "Book in seconds"

4. **Offers** — Monthly Plan + Mix & Match (black cards)
   Caption: "Save with plans"

5. **Gift** — Gift QR code
   Caption: "Gift a haircut"

## How to Generate
1. Open the app in iPhone simulator or TestFlight
2. Navigate to each screen
3. Take screenshots (Cmd+S in simulator)
4. Resize to required dimensions if needed

## Notes
- Use real app content, not mockups
- Show the gold/light theme (not dark mode)
- Ensure text is readable at thumbnail size
- No marketing overlays on first 3 screenshots (Apple prefers clean UI)
